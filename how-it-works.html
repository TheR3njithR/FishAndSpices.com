Managed matching

A structured path from lead to introduction.

Fish & Spices reviews commercial intent and usable detail before considering a potential match. Parties remain responsible for due diligence and every transaction decision.

  1. 01Lead submission

    A buyer requirement or private seller availability summary is submitted.

  2. 02Commercial-purpose review

    We assess whether the lead describes a genuine and workable B2B purpose.

  3. 03Contact verification

    Contact and business information may be checked before further handling.

  4. 04Requirement or stock qualification

    Missing product, quantity, location, timing or evidence details may be requested.

  5. 05Potential match identification

    Relevant demand or supply may be considered; no match is guaranteed.

  6. 06Consent-based introduction

    Contact details are shared only when an introduction is appropriate and agreed.

  7. 07Independent checks

    Parties arrange sampling, inspection, document review and negotiation.

  8. 08Commercial agreement

    Contracts, payment, insurance and logistics are agreed directly by the parties.

  9. 09Outcome and feedback

    Where shared, outcomes help improve follow-up and future qualification.

Important limitation

Submission, review or introduction does not guarantee matching, supply, sale, quality, price, payment, delivery or a completed transaction.
