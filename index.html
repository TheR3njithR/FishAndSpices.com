A considered introduction process

How potential matching works.

Each lead moves through a structured review before any introduction is considered.

  1. 01Lead submitted

    A buyer requirement or seller stock listing begins the process.

  2. 02Commercial purpose reviewed

    We look for genuine business intent and a workable request.

  3. 03Details checked

    Requirement or stock information is checked for useful specificity.

  4. 04Potential matches identified

    Relevant counterparties may be considered based on the submitted details.

  5. 05Introduction made with consent

    Contact information is shared only as part of an agreed introduction.

  6. 06Parties conduct due diligence

    Participants verify, inspect and negotiate independently.

After an introduction

Contracts, payments, product inspection, licences, transport, insurance and logistics are independently agreed by the participating parties.
