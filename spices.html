Spice sourcing

Commercial specifications go beyond the spice name.

Grade, form, origin, moisture, quality parameters, packing and destination requirements shape each qualified sourcing conversation.

Sourcing scope

Spice categories

Categories indicate sourcing scope, not guaranteed stock or specifications. Each potential supply requires independent testing and agreement.

Black pepper

Define origin, variety, density, berry size, moisture, sterilisation and residue requirements.

Cardamom

State origin, grade, capsule size, colour, moisture, packing and laboratory parameters.

Turmeric

Specify finger or bulb, variety, curcumin, moisture, polish, whole or powder form and contaminants.

Ginger

Clarify fresh or dried form, origin, size, fibre, moisture, treatment and packing.

Chilli

Include variety, colour, heat level, stem status, aflatoxin, residue and microbiological limits.

Cumin and coriander

Define whole or ground form, purity, moisture, volatile oil, foreign matter and packing.

Nutmeg and mace

State form, grade, size, moisture, origin, aflatoxin needs and destination compliance.

Cloves and fennel

Specify grade, head or stem tolerance, size, volatile oil, moisture and cleaning standard.

Fenugreek

Set seed size, purity, colour, moisture, residue and packing expectations.

Powders and value-added

Describe mesh, formulation, sterilisation, private label, oil or oleoresin parameters and packaging.

Qualification essentials

Build a specification that can be assessed

Requirements vary by product and destination. Values should be supported by representative samples and current laboratory evidence.

Grade and form

State variety, origin preference, whole, cracked, ground, oil or oleoresin form and the relevant commercial grade.

Quality parameters

Set applicable moisture, density, size, curcumin, volatile oil, residue, microbiological and aflatoxin limits.

Packing and destination

Define quantity, pack size and material, private-label needs, destination and required certifications.
