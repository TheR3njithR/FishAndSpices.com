Program terms

Partner Network Terms (Summary)

These operational terms complement site-level legal terms and can be revised by platform administrators for compliance and fraud control.

1) Approved status required

Only ACTIVE partners can receive attribution credit and commission generation.

2) First-valid referral attribution

The first valid referral token captured within its active period is retained for partner crediting logic.

3) Event-ledger basis

Commissions are generated from qualified events only, with dedupe protection for repeated actions.

4) Fraud safeguards

Self-referral, duplicate identity signals, and suspicious velocity may flag records for review and hold payout progression.

5) Cooling and review periods

Some rewards enter pending states before becoming payable based on rule-defined cooling and approval requirements.

6) Payout governance

Payouts follow platform minimums, review controls, and audit logs. Failed payouts may return commissions to payable state.

7) Accurate representation

Partners must not misrepresent business terms, pricing commitments, regulatory endorsements, or guaranteed transactional outcomes.

8) Program changes

Commission rates, rule conditions, and eligibility criteria are configurable and may be updated with notice in admin channels.
