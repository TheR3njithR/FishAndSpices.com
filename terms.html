Platform terms

Terms for qualified lead introductions.

Effective August 15, 2026. Use of the platform means you understand its independent lead-generation role and your own due-diligence responsibilities.

Platform role

Fish & Spices collects and qualifies commercial leads, explores potential matching and may facilitate consent-based introductions. It does not own inventory, act as buyer or seller, hold client money, provide escrow, guarantee participants or become a party to contracts between users.

Accurate and lawful information

You must provide current, accurate information you are authorised to share. You must not submit false stock, impersonate another business, misrepresent licences or certifications, infringe rights, distribute malware or use the platform for unlawful activity.

No guaranteed outcome

A provisional reference, review, follow-up or introduction is not verification, acceptance or a guarantee of a match or transaction. Availability, specification, price, sample, inspection, licence, payment, logistics and delivery remain subject to independent agreement.

Independent due diligence

Users must verify identity, authority, registration, licences, product evidence, samples, laboratory and inspection reports, bank details and contract terms. Users should obtain appropriate legal, technical, customs, tax, banking and insurance advice.

Commercial arrangements

Participants independently negotiate and document product specifications, price, Incoterms, payment, inspection, risk, title, insurance, regulatory compliance, logistics, remedies and dispute terms. Fish & Spices is not responsible for performance of those arrangements.

Lead handling

Fish & Spices may decline, pause or close a lead, request clarification or stop an introduction where information is insufficient, inconsistent, unsafe or unsuitable. Seller information is not promised public publication.

Limitation

To the extent permitted by applicable law, the platform is provided on an as-available basis without warranties of accuracy, availability, merchantability, fitness or transaction outcome. Nothing excludes a liability that cannot lawfully be excluded.

Changes and contact

These terms may be updated as the platform develops. Material changes will be reflected by an updated effective date. Questions may be sent through the Contact page.
