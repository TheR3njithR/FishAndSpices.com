Fish & seafood

Commercial sourcing starts with the species and form.

Scientific identity, size, condition, cold chain, quantity and destination materially affect whether a requirement or stock position can be qualified.

Sourcing scope

Fish and seafood categories

These are sourcing categories, not inventory claims. Availability, quality and credentials must be checked for each potential introduction.

Varal / snakehead

Specify scientific name, farmed or wild source, size range, form and destination requirements.

Karimeen / pearl spot

Clarify origin, harvest method, live or chilled requirement, grading and transport conditions.

Marine fish

Identify species, landing or processing location, catch evidence, form, freezing and packing.

Freshwater fish

State farmed preference, harvest window, size, whole or processed form and delivery conditions.

Shrimp and prawns

Include species, count size, treatment, glazing, block or IQF form and laboratory requirements.

Crab

Define species, live or processed format, size, packing, cold chain and destination regulations.

Fresh and chilled fish

Temperature control, harvest or catch timing, ice, packing and transit time require clear agreement.

Frozen seafood

Set freezing method, glazing, storage temperature, pack format and minimum shelf life.

Dried fish

Describe species, drying method, moisture, salt, packing and microbiological requirements.

Value-added seafood

Provide formulation, processing, portion, coating, private-label, packing and certification needs.

Qualification essentials

Details that improve a potential match

A common name alone may be ambiguous. Precise commercial details reduce unsuitable introductions and support independent due diligence.

Identity and size

Use the scientific name where possible and state the acceptable size or weight range and grading tolerance.

Form and cold chain

State whole, gutted, fillet, frozen, chilled, live or processed form together with temperature and storage requirements.

Volume and destination

Define quantity, recurring requirement, destination port or city, required date, packing and applicable import conditions.
