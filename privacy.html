Privacy notice

Purpose-limited handling of lead information.

Effective August 15, 2026. This notice describes the Phase 2 static intake workflow and information a user chooses to send.

Information you provide

Buyer and seller workflows request identity, business contact, location, commercial purpose, product, quantity, timing, specification, capability, documentation and declaration information. Contact messages may include a provisional lead reference and supporting context.

How the browser workflow works

The form validates entries and creates a provisional reference and review summary in the current page. It does not permanently store form data in local storage, session storage, cookies or a browser database. Information leaves the page only when you choose WhatsApp or email and complete sending in that service.

Purposes

Information received may be used to review commercial purpose, verify contact details, qualify requirements or availability, identify potential matches, request clarification, make consent-based introductions, handle safety concerns and record outcomes.

Sharing

Relevant information may be shared with a potential counterparty only where an introduction is considered appropriate and consent is obtained. Service providers involved in communications may process data under their own terms. Information may be disclosed where required by law or needed to address fraud, abuse or safety.

Retention and security

Received lead records may be retained for qualification, follow-up, safety, legal and operational purposes for a proportionate period. No internet transmission is completely secure. Do not send passwords, complete banking credentials or unnecessary sensitive personal data.

Your choices

You may ask about, correct or request deletion of information held by Fish & Spices, subject to legal, safety and recordkeeping needs. Contact us through the Contact page.

International communications

Commercial introductions may involve participants and communication providers in different countries. Users should consider applicable privacy and business-record requirements.
